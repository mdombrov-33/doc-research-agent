Manager FAQ: 2026 People Policy Changes

Published: 2025-12-18

What changed about annual leave?

The current handbook raises annual leave to 24 days and the carryover limit to 8 days from 1 January 2026. Managers should not use the 20-day allowance or 5-day carryover limit printed in the archived 2024 handbook.

What is the current expense deadline?

New expenses must be submitted within 30 calendar days. The 45-day period applies only to expenses governed by the archived handbook and should not be quoted as the current deadline.

How often do remote employees visit an office?

Remote-designated employees normally attend a Northstar office at least 3 days each calendar month. This office-presence rule is separate from the 45-day annual limit on cross-border remote work.

What is the learning allowance?

The 2026 professional-learning allowance is 1500 Northstar Credits per employee for the calendar year. Managers approve the learning plan before purchase, and the allowance is unrelated to the three-year home-office equipment budget.

When does family leave eligibility begin?

Paid family leave eligibility begins after 90 calendar days of employment. For foreseeable leave, managers should direct employees to People Operations at least 30 calendar days before the expected start.

When do regional benefits begin?

An employee must be assigned to a qualifying hub for 30 consecutive days before regional benefits begin. The waiting period applies again after a move to another hub and is not the same as family leave eligibility.
