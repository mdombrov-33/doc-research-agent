Northstar Labs Employee Handbook 2024

Published: 2023-12-15

Annual leave

For the 2024 and 2025 calendar years, full-time employees receive 20 annual leave days. Employees may carry no more than 5 unused days into the following calendar year; any amount above that limit expires.

Expense submission

Employees must submit reimbursable business expenses within 45 calendar days of the purchase date. Claims submitted after that deadline require approval from the finance operations lead.
